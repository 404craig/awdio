# Garden planning notes

These notes collect the plan for the spring beds, what worked last year, and the order in which the work should happen once the soil is workable again.

## What worked last year

- Sweet peas along the south fence, sown early and pinched out at four leaves.
- A thick mulch of leaf mould on the shady border, which kept the ferns happy through August.
- Watering in the early morning rather than the evening, which cut the slug damage noticeably.

## Order of work

Start with the structural jobs while the plants are dormant: edging, paths and the new raised bed by the shed. Then lift and divide the crowded perennials, and finally sow the annuals in modules under cover so they are ready to plant out after the last frost.

> The best time to plant a tree was twenty years ago. The second best time is now.

### Open questions

Whether to move the compost bins closer to the kitchen, and whether the old apple tree needs a proper winter prune or just the dead wood taken out.
